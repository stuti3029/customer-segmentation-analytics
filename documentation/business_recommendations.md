# Business Recommendations — Luna7 Clothing Rental Pilot

## Decision context

The question is whether a fictional fashion retailer should launch a clothing-rental offer to everyone at once, or start with carefully selected customers and learn from observed adoption.

This recommendation summary refers to the **two-cluster and regression version** of the coursework. No recommendation below has been implemented or measured in a real company.

## Recommendation 1 — Pilot with digitally engaged customers

**Rationale:** Customers exhibiting greater online purchasing activity, digital engagement and spend were interpreted as a more promising audience for early rental communications.

**Proposed actions:**

- Run a small rental pilot with customers meeting clearly defined eligibility criteria.
- Test informative digital campaigns, rental-plan descriptions and simple online onboarding.
- Compare an invitation group with a suitable baseline/control where feasible.

**Evaluation KPIs:** Invitation-to-trial conversion, first-rental completion, repeat rental, contribution margin per rental and customer feedback.

## Recommendation 2 — Tailor digital messaging

**Rationale:** The coursework regression identified positive *associations* between rental intention and online purchasing behaviour, Facebook engagement and recent expenditure.

**Proposed actions:**

- Test communication variants focused on convenience, style choice, value and sustainability.
- Use channel and customer-group reporting to learn which messages resonate.
- Keep campaign targeting proportionate and respectful of privacy/consent requirements.

**Evaluation KPIs:** Click-through rate, qualified sign-ups, cost per trial and actual rentals (rather than engagement alone).

## Recommendation 3 — Design a different journey for lower-engagement customers

**Rationale:** The second group was characterised by lower digital and purchasing engagement. Sending identical digital rental promotions to both groups may be inefficient.

**Proposed actions:**

- Explore customer concerns about price, trust, hygiene, convenience and return policies.
- Offer clear rental-service explanations and alternative discovery channels.
- Defer heavy promotional investment until pilot evidence supports it.

**Evaluation KPIs:** Awareness change, trial interest, conversion and satisfaction.

## Recommendation 4 — Validate the business case with real behaviour

The current analysis is based on a hypothetical retailer and measures **rental intention**, not actual purchases. Any commercial launch would require:

1. A testable value proposition and pilot budget.
2. Observed customer sign-ups, rentals, repeat purchases and returns.
3. Unit economics covering cleaning, handling, delivery, inventory depreciation and returns.
4. Segment-level contribution margin and retention reporting.
5. A decision gate for scaling, changing or stopping the pilot.

## What the study does *not* show

- That any proposed campaign increased sales or profits.
- That the two clusters will remain stable in a new customer population.
- That digital engagement *causes* rental adoption.
- That expressed rental intention guarantees commercially viable demand.

## Portfolio takeaway

The value of the analysis is turning a segmentation exercise into a **measurable commercial experiment**. A credible analyst recommends both an action and the metrics needed to test whether the action worked.
